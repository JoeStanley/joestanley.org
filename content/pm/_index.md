>
> [Gregor Anghel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comrade_Detective)

This syllabus is for you to work through on your own, at your own pace. Nobody runs it for you and nobody checks your work. It complements what you learn on the job. It gives you the principles behind the work, drawn mostly from other fields, so you can reason about situations nobody has shown you yet.

## How it works

- **Eight modules, one question each.** A module takes up to 90 minutes: up to an hour of reading, watching or playing, and about half an hour applying it to your own work.
- **The short path is free to read.** Books are on the full path, which is optional. Take it when a question keeps coming up in your work.
- **Every source says why to trust it** and what only it gives you, so you can decide what is worth your time.
- **Concept cards are one page each.** Keep them near your work.
- **On the site, your progress stays in your browser.** Nothing is sent anywhere.
