# Glossary

Each term marked in the text links here. Select one on the site to see its definition in place.

- **Constraint (bottleneck):** The one part of a system that limits what the whole system can deliver.
- **Feedback loop:** A chain of cause and effect that runs from the level of a stock back to one of its flows. A balancing loop pulls the stock toward a goal. A reinforcing loop pushes it further the way it is already going.
- **Leverage point:** A place in a system where a small change can produce a large change in how the whole behaves. Donella Meadows ranked twelve of them.
- **OKRs (objectives and key results):** A goal-setting format many teams use. Each objective is paired with a few measurable key results.
- **Stock:** Anything that builds up or drains away and can be counted or measured at one moment, such as a backlog or a bank balance. Only the flows into and out of it change it.
- **Work in progress (WIP):** Work that has been started and not finished. A long queue of it in front of one step is the usual sign of a constraint.
